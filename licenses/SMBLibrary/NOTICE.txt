FileHub uses SMBLibrary unmodified, as a separate dynamically loaded assembly, to browse
SMB2/3 shares. It is not linked into FileHub's own assemblies, so you may replace
SMBLibrary.dll next to the FileHub executable with your own build of the library.

Corresponding source for version 1.5.8.1: the project URL above (tag 1.5.8.1), or the
SMBLibrary package on nuget.org.
